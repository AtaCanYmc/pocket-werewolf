# Pocket Werewolf - Roles & Strategies Guide 📜

A complete reference guide for all playable roles, night actions, win conditions, and gameplay strategies in **Pocket Werewolf**.

---

## 🟢 The Good Team (Villagers)

**Win Condition:** Eliminate all members of the Werewolf pack.

---

### 👨‍🌾 Villager
- **Night Action:** None (Sleeps peacefully).
- **Abilities:** Participates in day discussions and votes in town trials.
- **Strategy Tip:** Watch voting patterns closely. Werewolves often hesitate to vote for pack members early on.

---

### 🔮 Seer
- **Night Action:** Selects one player per night to uncover their true allegiance.
- **Abilities:** Receives an immediate psychic vision revealing whether the target is Good or Evil.
- **Strategy Tip:** Do not reveal your identity on Day 1 unless you have discovered a Werewolf; otherwise, werewolves will target you on Night 2.

---

### 💉 Doctor
- **Night Action:** Chooses one player each night to protect with medical aid.
- **Abilities:** If the protected player is attacked by werewolves during the night, they survive. The Doctor may protect themselves.
- **Strategy Tip:** Try to predict who the Werewolves consider the biggest threat (e.g. vocal players or confirmed Seers).

---

### 🧙‍♀️ Witch
- **Night Action:** Wakes up to brew potent potions.
- **Abilities:** Has a single-use Poison Potion capable of eliminating a suspect during the night.
- **Strategy Tip:** Save your poison until you have strong clues or confirmed information from discussion.

---

### 🏹 Hunter
- **Night Action:** None.
- **Abilities:** If killed (either attacked at night or lynched by vote), the Hunter fires a parting shot to eliminate one player of their choice.
- **Strategy Tip:** Threaten suspects with your shot during intense debates to force werewolves into exposing inconsistencies.

---

### 🛡️ Mason
- **Night Action:** None.
- **Abilities:** Masons know who each other are at the start of the game, confirming 100% trust between them.
- **Strategy Tip:** Work together during daytime trials to lead town votes with verified innocence.

---

## 🔴 The Evil Team (Werewolves)

**Win Condition:** Equal or outnumber the living Villagers.

---

### 🐺 Werewolf
- **Night Action:** Wakes up each night to choose a victim with fellow wolves.
- **Abilities:** Blends into the village during the day and participates in trials.
- **Strategy Tip:** Create plausible alibis and avoid defending fellow werewolves too aggressively when the town is suspicious.

---

### 🌙 Dream Wolf
- **Night Action:** Sleeps at night while ordinary werewolves hunt.
- **Abilities:** If all ordinary werewolves die, the Dream Wolf awakens with full werewolf powers.
- **Strategy Tip:** Play like an innocent villager to survive late into the game as a sleeper agent.

---

### ✨ Sorceress
- **Night Action:** Searches for the Seer at night.
- **Abilities:** Learns if a target is the Seer, aiding the werewolf hunting pack.
- **Strategy Tip:** Discreetly guide werewolf votes during the day toward the Seer once discovered.

---

### 🦇 Minion
- **Night Action:** Knows who the werewolves are, but werewolves do not know who the minion is.
- **Abilities:** Works to derail trials, draw suspicion away from werewolves, and sacrifice themselves if needed.
- **Strategy Tip:** Act suspiciously enough to get lynched in place of a werewolf, protecting the true killers.
